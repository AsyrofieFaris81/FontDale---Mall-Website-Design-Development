About FontDale

A premium mall concept built around modern urban routines.

FontDale was envisioned by Franz Diego, first conceptualized in 2023, and officially opened in 2025 at Arcadia Circle.

2023 Concept began 2025 Official opening 2 Main destination wings

Our story

From a mall concept into a city lifestyle destination.

FontDale brings together global fashion brands, lifestyle stores, diverse dining experiences, and entertainment within two main wings: North Gallery and South Pavilion, connected by the signature Lumen Skywalk.

Founder

Franz Diego

Franz Diego imagined FontDale as a premium place that feels practical, social, and visually memorable. His direction focuses on comfort, strong tenant curation, clean navigation, and an atmosphere that supports both quick visits and longer experiences.

Premium designUrban lifestyleVisitor comfort

What makes FontDale unique

Three areas, one clear visitor journey.

Rather than creating scattered sections, FontDale uses the mall structure itself as a browsing story.

Fashion and lifestyle

North Gallery

A polished retail wing for fashion, beauty, footwear, wellness, and lifestyle shopping.

Dining and social

South Pavilion

A warmer wing for restaurants, cafes, family dining, and casual meetups.

Connection and identity

Lumen Skywalk

The signature connection route that helps users move between wings while enjoying a landmark visual moment.
